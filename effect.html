Global Climate Effects

Global warming increases heatwaves, heat stress, and reduces agricultural productivity worldwide.
Melting polar ice raises sea levels, threatening coastal cities and island nations.
Floods, droughts, storms, and wildfires are becoming more frequent and destructive.
Ecosystems collapse as species lose habitats and face extinction.

Indonesia

Highly vulnerable to climate change

Hover / Tap

Impacts in Indonesia

Increased rainfall causes frequent floods and landslides, damaging homes and infrastructure.
Northern Java experiences severe tidal flooding due to sea-level rise and land subsidence.
Coral bleaching and ocean warming weaken fisheries and marine tourism.
Irregular rainfall reduces clean water availability for cities and agriculture.

Long-Term Risks

Socio - Economic Effects

Farmers and fishers face declining income due to climate shocks.

Unequal Impacts

Wealthy nations emit the most, while poor communities suffer more.
Coastal and indigenous communities face higher climate risks.
Limited funding slows adaptation in developing regions.
Vulnerable voices are often excluded from climate decisions.
